# Paper

Import `Paper` from `bench-design` and load `bench-design/styles.css` once.

## Usage

Use a document sheet for caller-owned text and headings. Compose references separately. Do provide a textual cue such as “Highlighted passage” alongside a mark; avoid conveying significance through color alone. No extraction or document viewing behavior is provided.

## Accessibility

Paper adds no landmark or heading level. Native mark content remains available to assistive technology, but highlighting may not be announced; supply accompanying text. Interactive children keep their own keyboard behavior.
