# Document library
An editorial import area, searchable collection and modal reading sheet composed entirely from shared primitives. GridList owns each document’s border and activation; compose its content directly to avoid nesting a second bordered Card. Paper previews highlight a short excerpt within surrounding text.

Supply document data, categories, search policy, upload, extraction, persistence and routing. All documents here are fictional. Choosing accepted files opens an import dialog with a sample action, refusal feedback and a simulated queue. Completed entries join the fictional collection and can open their reading sheet. Files are neither uploaded nor read; progress and recognition are demonstrations. Reduced motion completes the queue immediately.

Tab to search, type a title, then Tab to the document-type controls. Arrow keys move between facets; Space selects. Tab into the collection, use arrows to navigate and Enter to open the reading sheet. Escape dismisses it and returns focus to the activated row. The sheet fills the viewport on small screens. Use the theme selector for light and dark presentations.
