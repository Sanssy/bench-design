# Timeline

Import `Timeline` from `bench-design` and load `bench-design/styles.css` once.

## Usage

Use a vertical timeline for a caller-ordered sequence of events or steps.
Markers are visible text: dates, open periods and unknown dates are all caller-owned.
The component never parses dates or sorts items.

- Do provide a descriptive list label and stable, unique item identifiers.
- Do compose rich titles and content, including Link or ActionList when available.
- Avoid making the entire event interactive or using the rail to convey status.
- Keep content responsive; fixed-width embedded media must handle its own sizing.

## Accessibility

A named native ordered list preserves the supplied order. Its rail and square
markers are decorative CSS; textual markers remain available to assistive technology.
No heading level is imposed on titles. The timeline itself has no keyboard behavior;
links and other interactive children retain their own keyboard interaction and focus.
